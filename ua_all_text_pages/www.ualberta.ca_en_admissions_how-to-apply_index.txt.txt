View key dates & deadlines dates-deadlines/index.html Program Choices + Application Fees New to the University Students If you have never attended the University of Alberta, you have the opportunity to select up to two program choices per application. You will first be evaluated for your first-choice program. If you are eligible for your first-choice program, you will receive an offer for that program, and you will not be evaluated for your second-choice program, regardless of whether you choose to accept your first offer or not. You will be evaluated for your second-choice program only if you are not eligible for your first-choice program. Current and Previous U of A Students For students who are currently attending the University of Alberta or have previously attended the University of Alberta, only one program choice per application is permitted. If you wish to be considered for more than one program, an application and application fee are required for each program choice. A nonrefundable application fee is charged for each Application for Admission and Readmission or Internal Transfer form submitted. Online applications are not successfully submitted until the online payment process is completed. The University of Alberta strictly enforces its policy of withholding processing of applications until the appropriate fee has been received. The application fee is a mandatory fee and we do not grant application fee waivers upon request.
