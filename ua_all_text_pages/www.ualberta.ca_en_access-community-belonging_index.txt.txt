about/meet-the-vice-provost.html History about/history.html ACCESS, COMMUNITY + BELONGING Building and sustaining a vibrant and interconnected university requires that we tell a different and transformative story. Now, more than any other time in our history, we have the energy and know-how to create this story. We encourage and support individual, collaborative and collective efforts to protect human rights and identify, address, and reduce inequities and barriers to access. We welcome and seek to enable community members’ contributions toward mutuality, reciprocity, responsibility and interdependence. We work together to integrate and enact these core principles in all that we do as we strive to build and sustain our vibrant and interconnected university community. The Disability Cultures and Access Hub (DCA Hub) is a database of U of A resources that can help students, faculty and staff with navigating disability-related barriers, register for and implement accommodations, improve accessibility and engage with disability cultures. Go to the DCA Hub services/disabilities-hub.html Learn more about Disability Cultures + Access initiatives/disability-cultures-access.html Shaping a more accessible and relational university community Rooted in the university’s deep commitment to community engagement and transformative collaboration, we work toward a university community where all have access to success and enjoy a meaningful sense of belonging. As we navigate the challenges of the past few years and learn from our work we look toward the future with critical hope. Our action plan aims to unite and disunify expertise in diverse approaches and disciplines across institutional and unit plans, ensuring that our strategic priorities are integrated, interdisciplinary and dynamic. Universities in Times of Crisis: Conversations across Differences This event series, spearheaded by the Office of the Vice-Provost in collaboration with portfolios across campus, addresses the role post-secondary institutions play in times of crises and societal transformation. Beginning with an initial period of 12 months, this series aims to enhance skills and build capacity within the U of A community, fostering spaces of care, courage and resilience across differences. Impact + Effect Strategic renewal for EDI at the University of Alberta shifts the conversation to prioritizing the effect and outcome of this work. Extensive consultations underscored the need to tell a new story, and the words that were used most provide a clear path forward. The words access, community and belonging (ACB) offer us a common purpose associated with the overarching strategy.
