A Welcome to North Campus Our original campus blends the historic with cutting edge: the University of Alberta’s North Campus is located in the province's capital of Edmonton, on the edge of the North Saskatchewan River valley. It covers 50 city blocks, with abundant green spaces among its 150 buildings.
