transform into remarkable realities. From accelerating solutions in energy, climate transition and food sustainability to forging new paths in artificial intelligence, health and Indigenous leadership, U of A expertise is sought out on today’s most pressing local and global issues. United with partners in industry, academia and government, we are looking to the future – and leading with purpose.
