The scope of practice for pharmacists in Alberta is the broadest in the world. Alberta pharmacists can provide provision of health advice, precision medicine, disease screening, injections and vaccinations, prescriptions and referrals. We put our people first. Our students, researchers, preceptors, alumni and donors are our biggest strength. We put them first so they can put their patients and communities first. Our Faculty excels in transferring its research to the marketplace. Gain your MSc or PhD that brings life-saving research to the real world, and in one of Canada's best emerging biotech and drug development landscapes.
