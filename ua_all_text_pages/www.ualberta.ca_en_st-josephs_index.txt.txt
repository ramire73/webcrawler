Learn More » https://www.ualberta.ca/en/st-josephs/newsandeventspage/news.html SJC's New Diploma in Liberal Arts APPLICATIONS OPEN The Diploma in Liberal Arts is a new two-year program, starting in September 2027, that focuses on interdisciplinary learning, critical thinking, and effective communication. Students who successfully complete the Diploma are guaranteed entrance into their 3rd year of studies in the Faculty of Arts at the University of Alberta, with pathways to MacEwan University and other Alberta universities. Build the skills and abilities to thrive in further post-secondary studies, in your future career, and in your personal life. Be part of a welcoming community that is rooted in Catholic engagement with the world and discern for yourself how to be human in a complex world. Dr. Kostelecky has been at SJC since 2009, serving as Associate Dean, and then as VPAD since 2020. Learn More » about/office-of-the-president.html Dr. Shawn Flynn Reappointed as President of St. Joseph's College, University of Alberta for a Second Five-year Term EDMONTON - September 2, 2025 St. Joseph’s College Board of Governors is pleased to announce President Flynn has been renewed for a second five-year term, 2027-2032. Flynn will complete his first five-year term on June 30, 2026, and then take a well-earned 12-month sabbatical from July 1, 2026, to June 30, 2027, before starting his second term as SJC President.
