Take virtual tour https://www.massinteract.com/university-of-alberta/ Top 4 university in Canada Top 100 university in the world 97% Graduate Employment Rate How to apply to the U of A Applying to university doesn’t have to be complicated — here’s a quick overview of the next steps you need to take. Applications close March 1 for most programs. Apply early for your best chance of admission. Step 1: Choose your campus Step 2: Choose your program(s) Step 3: Submit your application Step 4: Apply for scholarships Step 5: Learn about application evaluation Step 6: Check your status Step 7: Accept your offer and apply for your study permit Step 8: Register for courses Start your application https://apply.ualberta.ca/apply/ Scholarships and Funding Over $53M in undergraduate awards available each year We offer two types of awards: admission-based and application-based: Admission-Based You are automatically assessed for multiple awards at the time of admission. Eligibility is based on your admission average at the time of your admission offer, so you can receive a scholarship you don’t even have to apply for! Application-Based We use a single application form to match you to scholarships and awards worth up to CAN $120,000. These are assessed based on your background, experiences, academics and interests. You can apply to these awards after you have submitted your U of A application. Have questions? Our international student recruiters are here to help you navigate the admissions process.
