Indigenous Initiatives » about/indigenous-initiatives.html Health, Safety + Environment » about/health-safety-environment.html Faculty of Law The University of Alberta’s Faculty of Law is Western Canada’s oldest law school and one of Canada’s leading legal education and research institutions — regularly earning the ranking as one of Canada’s top five law schools. When you become a student in our faculty, you become part of a longstanding tradition of excellence in legal education. At the same time, our faculty is committed to growth and evolution — we are comfortable shedding traditions if they do not align with our current values. Our programs are built on a supportive culture that provides students with hands-on learning experiences designed to prepare them for a successful career in a rapidly changing legal profession. With a team of dedicated faculty who are experts in their field, students are taught exactly what they need to know to excel in whatever career they pursue. Top 5 law schools in Canada 82% of students participate in experiential learning $2M in student scholarships and bursaries each year 96% articling placement rate by convocation about/strategic-plan.html Faculty of Law Strategic Plan 2025‑2030 This strategic plan reflects both who we are today and who we aspire to be as a Faculty of Law. At its heart is a commitment to being a true home for our community, a place where students, faculty, staff, alumni and community and industry partners feel valued, supported, and inspired to thrive. Our JD Program programs/juris-doctor/index.html Graduate Studies The Faculty of Law also offers a one-year master's and a three-year doctorate program. Both the master’s and doctorate programs are research degrees involving advanced research and analysis. Our Graduate Programs programs/graduate/index.html Research + Impact Our faculty is a dynamic group of scholars, working at the cutting edge of legal research both within Canada and globally. Their high-impact research contributes to academic knowledge, legal practice and decision-making and law reform and policy.
