../index.html Online Courses Online On-Demand Courses Online on-demand courses, also known as Massive Open Online Courses (MOOCs), are engaging courses that you can take at any time. The University of Alberta has partnered with Coursera https://www.coursera.org/partners/ualberta to develop on-demand rigorous versions of these courses to the general public as well as for-credit for U of A students and potentially for other students in other institutions. On-Demand Courses + Alberta Curriculum Alignment The U of A is proud to offer asynchronous open online courses to the community. At home or on the go, these courses are a great resource for keeping ourselves and our families learning and having fun.
