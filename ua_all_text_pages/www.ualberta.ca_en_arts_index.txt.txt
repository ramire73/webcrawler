Arts Impact Stories alumni-and-giving/arts-impact-stories.html Faculty of Arts The Faculty of Arts proudly provides a welcoming, inclusive atmosphere for our multi-talented students to thrive. Our first-class programs open doors to rewarding careers in hundreds of diverse fields, as demonstrated by our prestigious alumni who have gone on to become leading change-makers and sought-after experts around the world. The value and power of an Arts degree is unmatched. Whether you’re interested in the Fine Arts, Humanities or Social Sciences, we have a program and a path of study waiting for you. Our award-winning and friendly faculty members, staff and advisors can’t wait to meet you and help you reach your goals. Prospective Students Explore our undergraduate and graduate programs by visiting our newly updated program guides that outline your journey from now until graduation. We also offer skill-focused certificate programs. Events Confronting Colonial Legacies: A Student Gathering Nov. 20, 2026 9:30 AM - 2:00 PM MST University Commons Led by Indigenous Elders, students, and scholars, this small gathering will create space to learn about the histories and ongoing legacies of residential schools and consider the responsibilities we carry in advancing truth and reconciliation. The gathering will include Elder teachings, a shared meal, facilitated conversation, and a hands-on art activity led by Cree artist Tamara Blesse, offering students an opportunity to reflect on and process what they have learned.
