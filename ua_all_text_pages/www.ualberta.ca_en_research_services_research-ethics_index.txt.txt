About The Research Ethics Office (REO) oversees and manages the process of reviewing and monitoring research involving human participants, animal research and US financial conflicts of interest ../develop-and-submit-proposal/financial-conflict-of-interest-disclosures.html . The REO is part of the Research Integrity Support Portfolio of the Office of the Vice-President, Research.
