of Alberta Library. Serving the University community and the general public, our vast collections include over 7 million titles and more than 10 million volumes in both print and electronic formats. Our resources support regional, national, and international scholarly needs. With thousands of scholarly journals, comprehensive reference resources and unique special collections focused on the Canadian prairies and beyond, we are committed to providing materials that meet both current and future research and teaching needs at the University of Alberta.
