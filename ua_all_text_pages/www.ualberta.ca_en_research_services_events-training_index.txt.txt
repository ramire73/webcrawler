Workshops and seminars address specific needs in research proposal development and funding administration. Topics include Pre-Engagement (building Community relationships and co-creating research with communities), Indigenous Data Sovereignty (Indigenous control of data and sovereignty principles), and Publishing and dissemination (Accessibility, cultural protocols, and community empowerment).
