Uplifting the whole people Guided by Forward Together: The University of Alberta People Strategy 2024-2034 https://www.ualberta.ca/en/human-resources-health-safety-environment/culture-and-well-being/culture/people-strategy/index.html , we have established a clear set of values to serve as long-term guiding lights alongside our mission and vision. These values provide a common basis for our work, decision-making and personal connection to the university. While the mission and vision describe what we aim to achieve, our values describe how we collectively behave and make decisions to reach those goals. They will align with strategic goals and provide a framework for consistent decision-making across the entire organization. Unwavering Curiosity Shared Accountability A Spirit of Community Impact through Excellence Our values are embodied in the words of our university's first president. ... knowledge shall not be the concern of scholars alone. The uplifting of the whole people shall be its final goal. Henry Marshall Tory Living our Values The overarching statement from Henry Marshall Tory inspires us to ensure that the knowledge created within the university is guided by the aspiration for a broader benefit: a tangible, positive effect on the wider community. The uplifting of the community, the whole people, is the core. These are more than just words on a page. They serve as our long-term guiding lights — steering our organization and giving us a shared foundation for every decision we make and every project we undertake. The descriptions below clarify the values and ensure we remain consistent in our approach, helping us stay focused on what truly matters as we work together and move in the same direction. Unwavering Curiosity devoted to Quaecumque vera It is the spark behind every courageous question, big breakthrough and bold new idea. We value the pursuit of knowledge and the continuous asking of new questions in our labs, libraries and lecture halls. Inspiration behind the art A Spirit of Community, grounded in caring and respect Where voices gather, belonging grows — and together we go further. We are active partners with communities locally and globally, because the work we do together reaches further than anything we could accomplish alone. Inspiration behind the art Shared Accountability for a sustainable future What we’re given, we honour — preserving possibility for generations. We act as custodians for the long-term well-being of society, our planet, our culture and the university’s legacy. Inspiration behind the art The tireless pursuit of Impact through Excellence in all we do Success is measured not only by what we create but also by the lives changed because of it. We translate knowledge into action to solve real-world problems, creating discoveries that ripple outward and shape a stronger future. Inspiration behind the art × Unwavering Curiosity An abstract eye symbolizes how curiosity opens our perspective and invites deeper exploration of big ideas. × A Spirit of Community Overlapping faces show people coming together and sharing a common purpose. × Shared Accountability Repeating arrows show shared directions and responsibility for the future. × Impact through Excellence Radiating shapes illustrate how ideas and actions spread outward and affect the world beyond their starting point. Our Values Journey This foundational work began by reviewing thoughtful input from previous consultations, including the Shape: A Strategic Plan of Impact https://www.ualberta.ca/en/strategic-plan/index.html , Igniting Purpose: Student Experience Action Plan (SEAP) https://www.ualberta.ca/en/strategic-plan/student-experience-action-plan/index.html , and Braiding Past, Present and Future: University of Alberta Indigenous Strategic Plan https://www.ualberta.ca/en/indigenous/strategic-plan/index.html . Over the past year, this was supplemented by focused discussions to translate shared ideas into value terms. This phased engagement culminated in our What We Heard report https://www.ualberta.ca/en/human-resources-health-safety-environment/media-library/culture-well-being/orgvalues-whatweheard.pdf and targeted discussions, including “values labs,” online surveys and ongoing outreach.
