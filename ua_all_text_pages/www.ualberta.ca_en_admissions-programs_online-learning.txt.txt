Online Learning Online Learning Welcome to Online Learning at the University of Alberta Online Learning Welcome to Online Learning at the University of Alberta The University of Alberta is investing in purpose-built online post-secondary education—rooted in innovative digital pedagogies, world-class faculty, exceptional design, and a championed student experience. The University of Alberta is driven to create immersive and accessible learning opportunities that reshape how our students learn, grow and contribute to society. Purpose-built online courses and programs in areas of distinct strengths are in development, supporting a comprehensive range of credentials and pathways into post-secondary education, and shaping futures through top-tier online education. At our core, the U of A is driven to create greater access to learning opportunities. We are on a mission to unleash the power of digital learning and democratizing knowledge for all those who seek answers to their burning questions. Online Graduate Program Opportunities The U of A is continually expanding its online graduate program offerings with several NEW programs admitting students right now – and more opening soon! When it comes to choosing whether to take a certificate or a degree program, there is no right or wrong choice. Choosing between a graduate certificate and a master’s degree depends on your goals, schedule and preferred learning path. Both options offer valuable opportunities to advance your career. These graduate degrees will allow students to gain post-graduate expertise that will give you greater employability and flexibility. Whether starting your career, growing in a role or pivoting to a new direction, a graduate credential adds valuable knowledge and increases momentum towards achieving your career goals. Course-Based Master's https://www.ualberta.ca/en/graduate-programs/index.html#sort=%40ua__grad_sub%20ascending&f:@ua__program_degrees=[Master's]&f:@ua__program_mode=[Online] Course-based master’s degrees are increasingly valued in today’s global economy. These programs typically include more courses than thesis-based programs, providing expansive learning that will be utilized in completing the degree’s required capstone research project. Graduate Certificates https://www.ualberta.ca/en/graduate-programs/index.html#sort=%40ua__grad_sub%20ascending&f:@ua__program_degrees=[Certificate]&f:@ua__program_mode=[Online] A graduate certificate is a credential awarded to students who complete a specific set of four graduate-level courses in a concentrated professional area. In some cases, credits earned through a graduate certificate may be laddered or stacked into a master’s degree. These programs can be completed in less than a year, are available in-person or online and with part-time options and have multiple intakes each year.
