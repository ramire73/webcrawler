            Faculty of

            Engineering

            The Faculty of Engineering combines world-class academic programs with hands-on learning that empowers skilled undergraduate and graduate students to turn daring ideas into real-world applications that address complex global challenges.

            Through state-of-the-art facilities and cutting-edge research, we prepare the next generation of engineers to lead in an evolving technological landscape. Our commitment to transformative education ensures graduates are equipped to drive industry forward, translate discoveries into commercial ventures, and build resilient infrastructure for Alberta, Canada, and beyond.

            Our vision is to be the most daring engineering community on the planet. We are engineers united by our unwavering dedication to solving the world’s greatest challenges and building a better future for society.

            We are: Driven. Trailblazing. Curious. Supportive. Grounded. Considerate.

            Prospective Students

            The Faculty of Engineering offers 9 fully accredited undergraduate degrees and a wide range of graduate degrees. Your beginning starts here.
