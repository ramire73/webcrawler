English Language School

At the University of Alberta, we know that studying English as an additional language is more than learning words and phrases – it is a chance to explore interesting ideas, discover other cultures, and develop new communication skills that will take you around the world.

Going beyond classroom study!

Want to know the best part about studying at the English Language School?  Our Student Engagement Centre offers daily workshops, activities, and engagement experiences – all included in your tuition fee. Check out “student life” to read highlights of what is happening in SEC.

Winner of the ATESL, 2022 Carolyn Dieleman Award

Visit the Student Engagement Centre

The Student Journey

Daniel Navarro is an international student from Panama. He has successfully completed the English for Academic Purposes Program at the English Language School, starting from EAP 135 and finishing with EAP 500 online. Daniel has now entered graduate school in the Faculty of Engineering. This video reflects on his EAP program experience.

Quick Facts

Tuition is priced very competitively

Fair instruction fees which also include: medical insurance, health services, student services, and exclusive student engagement centre access fees.

Extensive teaching experience

Our instructors have extensive experience and training in English language instruction. All of our instructors are university graduates who have completed advanced training in Teaching English as a Second Language (TESL).

Located at the U of A Campus

The University of Alberta is located in the province's capital of Edmonton, on the edge of the North Saskatchewan River valley. The campus blends historic with cutting edge. It covers 50 city blocks with abundant green spaces among its 150 buildings.

Student Engagement Centre

Our unique recreational and study space for students to make friends, take part in extra language activities and engage with the community in Edmonton.

Homestay Services

The English Language School at The University of Alberta has partnered with Canada Homestay Network (CHN) to offer a unique all-inclusive housing option for our students. When you choose homestay with CHN, you become a member of the family you live with, part of the community you live in, and make the most of your time in Canada.

Territorial Acknowledgement

The University of Alberta, its buildings, labs and research stations are primarily located on the territory of Néhiyaw (Cree), Niitsitapi (Blackfoot), Métis, Nakoda (Stoney), Dene, Haudenosaunee (Iroquois) and Anishinaabe (Ojibway/Saulteaux), lands that are now known as part of Treaties 6, 7 and 8 and homeland of the Métis. The University of Alberta respects the sovereignty, lands, histories, languages, knowledge systems and cultures of all First Nations, Métis, and Inuit.
