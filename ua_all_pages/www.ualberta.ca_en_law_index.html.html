                                            Faculty of

                                            Law

                                            The University of Alberta’s Faculty of Law is Western Canada’s oldest law school and one of Canada’s leading legal education and research institutions — regularly earning the ranking as one of Canada’s top five law schools.

                                            When you become a student in our faculty, you become part of a longstanding tradition of excellence in legal education. At the same time, our faculty is committed to growth and evolution — we are comfortable shedding traditions if they do not align with our current values. Our programs are built on a supportive culture that provides students with hands-on learning experiences designed to prepare them for a successful career in a rapidly changing legal profession. With a team of dedicated faculty who are experts in their field, students are taught exactly what they need to know to excel in whatever career they pursue.

                                            Faculty of Law Strategic Plan

                                            2025‑2030

                                            This strategic plan reflects both who we are today and who we aspire to be as a Faculty of Law. At its heart is a commitment to being a true home for our community, a place where students, faculty, staff, alumni and community and industry partners feel valued, supported, and inspired to thrive.

                                            Juris Doctor

                                            The Juris Doctor (JD) is a three-year degree program where students receive a strong foundation in traditional areas of legal practice, while also exploring hands-on learning and emerging fields of specialization.

                                            Graduate Studies

                                            The Faculty of Law also offers a one-year master's and a three-year doctorate program. Both the master’s and doctorate programs are research degrees involving advanced research and analysis.

                                            Research + Impact

                                            Our faculty is a dynamic group of scholars, working at the cutting edge of legal research both within Canada and globally. Their high-impact research contributes to academic knowledge, legal practice and decision-making and law reform and policy.

                                            Territorial Acknowledgement

                                            The University of Alberta, its buildings, labs and research stations are primarily located on the territory of Néhiyaw (Cree), Niitsitapi (Blackfoot), Métis, Nakoda (Stoney), Dene, Haudenosaunee (Iroquois) and Anishinaabe (Ojibway/Saulteaux), lands that are now known as part of Treaties 6, 7 and 8 and homeland of the Métis. The University of Alberta respects the sovereignty, lands, histories, languages, knowledge systems and cultures of all First Nations, Métis, and Inuit.
