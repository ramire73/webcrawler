                                      Alberta School of

                                      Business

                                      As one of Canada’s Top 6 business schools and the country’s first and longest continually AACSB-accredited program, the Alberta School of Business prepares bold thinkers to shape tomorrow’s economy.

                                      With groundbreaking and world-leading research, innovative programs and an inclusive culture of collaboration and discovery, we empower students, faculty and partners to thrive — here in Edmonton and around the world.

                                      Programs

                                      Bachelor of Commerce (BCom)

                                      With our BCom, you’ll expand your horizons, gain valuable work experience and dive deep into an area of specialization. Apply directly from high school or as a post secondary transfer student.

                                      • Bachelor of Commerce
                                      • Bilingual BCom
                                      • BCom Co-op
                                      • BCom Honours
                                      • BCom After Degree

                                      Masters Programs

                                      Bringing together world-class faculty with the best and brightest students, the MBA and Master's programs at the University of Alberta equip you with the skills and knowledge to advance your career.

                                      • Full-time MBA
                                      • Part-time MBA
                                      • FastTrack MBA
                                      • Executive MBA
                                        • Master of Management Analytics (MMA)
                                        • Master of Accounting (MAcc)
                                        • Master of Real Estate Development (MRED)
                                        • Masters Programs in China

                                      Doctoral Program

                                      The Doctor of Philosophy in Business program is designed to prepare students for a research-focused career in academia in six specializations:

                                      • Accounting
                                      • Business Economics (NEW! Applications open Sept. 1)
                                      • Finance
                                      • Marketing
                                      • Operations + Information Systems
                                      • Strategy, Entrepreneurship + Management

                                      Executive Education Programs

                                      At the Alberta School of Business, we partner with established private and public-sector leaders across Western Canada to create meaningful, lasting change and drive organizational performance through impactful learning solutions.

                                      • Custom Programs
                                      • Directors Education Program
                                      • Executive Certificate in Municipal Leadership
                                      • UAcademy

                                      Study Abroad

                                      Whether you choose to embark on an outgoing exchange program or join one of our international study tours, you'll have the chance to immerse yourself in another country's business practices, languages and culture.

                                      Experiential Learning

                                      Research

                                      The Alberta School of Business is home to world renowned scholars who continually deepen our understanding of important issues facing business here in Canada and around the globe.

                                      Our faculty lead rigorous, interdisciplinary scholarship that advances knowledge and enriches teaching and learning across the school.

                                      Explore Research

                                      Centres + Institutes

                                      ASB’s centres and institutes focus expertise on the issues that matter most to business and society. These collaborative hubs connect faculty, students and industry partners to advance applied research, share evidence-based insight, and deepen our engagement with the business community. They play a key role in extending the school’s reach and relevance beyond the classroom.

                                      FEATURED INSTRUCTOR

                                      Vern Glaser

                                      As a 2025 teaching award winner for distinguished teaching in the MBA program, Vern Glaser draws on his expertise in entrepreneurship, strategy, and the application of emerging technologies such as AI and data analytics to create interactive, real-world learning experiences that challenge students to think critically, solve complex problems and present their ideas with confidence.
