The College of Natural and Applied Sciences brings together the scientific expertise across our faculties, enabling a new level of interdisciplinary research and innovative teaching practices.     Matina Kalcounis-Rueppell

    Matina Kalcounis-Rueppell is the college dean and vice-provost of the University of Alberta’s College of Natural and Applied Sciences, which brings together the combined strength of the faculties of agricultural, life and environmental sciences, engineering, and science.

    As interim dean of the College of Natural and Applied Sciences from 2021-2023, Kalcounis Rueppell's collaborative approach has helped faculty and staff to successfully navigate an extraordinary amount of change. Working in partnership with college leadership and the council of deans, she has helped to consolidate academic and administrative processes at the college level, encouraging closer collaboration between units.
