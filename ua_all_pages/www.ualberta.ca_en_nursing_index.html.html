The Faculty of Nursing offers undergraduate programs leading to a Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BScN) degree, a BSc.N bilingual/B.Sc.Inf. bilingue degree, or a BScN Honors degree. Students who complete these degrees are eligible to write the NCLEX Examination to become a Registered Nurse in Canada.

  • BScN Collaborative Program
  • BScN Bilingual Program / Programm B.Sc.Inf. bilingue
  • BScN Honors Program
  • BScN After Degree Program

Apply Now

Participating in a Faculty of Nursing graduate program will position you as an innovative and expert nurse leader who is ready to advance professional nursing roles in diverse and complex contexts. The Faculty of Nursing offers a Master of Nursing program and a PhD in Nursing program.

  • MN (Master of Nursing)
  • PhD (Doctoral Program)
  • MNPP (Advanced Clinical Focus)

Learn more about graduate programs

The Faculty of Nursing offers cutting-edge programs. We serve the community by advancing and sharing nursing knowledge through teaching, research and public service. To foster high-quality evidence-based nursing care the Faculty of Nursing welcomes donations from alumni, colleagues, friends, and industry partners for scholarships, bursaries, student resources, research and endowed research chairs.

NEW Round-Based Admissions Process

If you are a high school applicant to the BScN Collaborative or BScN Bilingual programs, your application will be evaluated in scheduled rounds. Once an offer is made, you have 30 days to accept it and pay the deposit (unless otherwise stated). If you do not accept within the 30-day window, your offer will be cancelled.
