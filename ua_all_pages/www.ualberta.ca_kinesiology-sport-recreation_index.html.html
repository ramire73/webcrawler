      Faculty of

      Kinesiology, Sport, and Recreation

      The Faculty of KSR provides solutions to improve well-being by advancing how we understand and participate in physical activity and leisure.

      Offering multiple undergraduate and graduate programs, KSR connects the art and science of human movement, performance, and play.

      KSR Research Impact Report 2024–25

      Explore this year’s breakthroughs, achievements, and research that is shaping the future of human movement, performance and play in our community and globally. This milestone edition reflects a year of global recognition and transformative research across KSR.

      How Jasper seniors are coping nearly two years after the wildfire

      Nearly two years after the Jasper wildfire, many seniors are still feeling its effects. A new University of Alberta podcast, called Flowers in the Ashes, is sharing their stories. The podcast explores how hobbies, recreation and community can support healing after a disaster. (CBC Radio Active)

      Hitting new heights: Sherwood Park's Abby Guezen named top uni athlete

      Student Abby Guezen (Kinesiology, Sport, and Recreation) received the Lois and Doug Mitchell award for outstanding sportsmanship, leadership and athletic achievement at the U SPORTS level | Sherwood Park News

      From the Mat to the Finish Line: Taylor McPherson’s Journey of Strength and Identity

      Graduate Taylor McPherson (Kinesiology, Sport, and Recreation), winner of The Amazing Race Canada Season 10, wrestled for six years with the U of A Pandas Wrestling program | Alberta Native News

      The Impacts of Weight Stigma

      U of A assistant professor discusses how weight stigma and negative comments can work against healthy behaviours: Taniya Nagpal (Kinesiology, Sport, and Recreation) explains that weight stigma is defined as negative misconceptions and stereotypes associated with weight, and can impact any body of any size or weight. Nagpal says her lab’s research shows that weight stigma impacts mental and physical health. CTV Edmonton Watch (video)
