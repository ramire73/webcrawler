          Centres + Institutes

          Representing a diverse range of research expertise, Centres and Institutes engage in scholarship and advance the mission of the University of Alberta through specific attention to particular areas of study or research.

          Centres and Institutes Advisory Task Force Report

          In 2024, a University of Alberta Centres and Institutes Advisory Task Force completed a report providing recommendations for institutional engagement with academic centres and institutes and their role in supporting the growth of the university’s research and education capacity.

          Territorial Acknowledgement

          The University of Alberta, its buildings, labs and research stations are primarily located on the territory of Néhiyaw (Cree), Niitsitapi (Blackfoot), Métis, Nakoda (Stoney), Dene, Haudenosaunee (Iroquois) and Anishinaabe (Ojibway/Saulteaux), lands that are now known as part of Treaties 6, 7 and 8 and homeland of the Métis. The University of Alberta respects the sovereignty, lands, histories, languages, knowledge systems and cultures of all First Nations, Métis, and Inuit.
