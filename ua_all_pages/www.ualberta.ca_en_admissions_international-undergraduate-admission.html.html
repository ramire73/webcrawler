Scholarships and Funding

Over $53M in undergraduate awards available each year

We offer two types of awards: admission-based and application-based:

Admission-Based

You are automatically assessed for multiple awards at the time of admission. Eligibility is based on your admission average at the time of your admission offer, so you can receive a scholarship you don’t even have to apply for!

Application-Based

We use a single application form to match you to scholarships and awards worth up to CAN $120,000. These are assessed based on your background, experiences, academics and interests. You can apply to these awards after you have submitted your U of A application.
