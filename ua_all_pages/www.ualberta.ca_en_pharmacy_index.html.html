Pharmacy and Pharmaceutical Sciences

Serving as Alberta's sole pharmacy school since 1914, we hold consistent rankings among the top three institutions in Canada, top 50 globally, and top 15 for global research. Join us today.

Programs

Entry to Practice PharmD

A four-year professional program/clinical doctorate consisting of coursework and practice experience.

PharmD for Practicing Pharmacists

An undergraduate post-professional doctorate that provides advanced education in patient care.

MBA and PharmD Combined Degrees

Canada's first combined Masters of Business Administration and Doctor of Pharmacy degree program.

MSc + PhD

Graduate degrees are available in both pharmacy and pharmaceutical sciences research areas.

Post-professional Pharmacy Education

Explore our post-professional certificates, the Diploma in Canadian Pharmacy Practice or standalone courses.

The scope of practice for pharmacists in Alberta is the broadest in the world.

Alberta pharmacists can provide provision of health advice, precision medicine, disease screening, injections and vaccinations, prescriptions and referrals.

We put our people first.

Our students, researchers, preceptors, alumni and donors are our biggest strength. We put them first so they can put their patients and communities first.

Our Faculty excels in transferring its research to the marketplace.

Gain your MSc or PhD that brings life-saving research to the real world, and in one of Canada's best emerging biotech and drug development landscapes.

    Territorial Acknowledgement

    The University of Alberta, its buildings, labs and research stations are primarily located on the territory of Néhiyaw (Cree), Niitsitapi (Blackfoot), Métis, Nakoda (Stoney), Dene, Haudenosaunee (Iroquois) and Anishinaabe (Ojibway/Saulteaux), lands that are now known as part of Treaties 6, 7 and 8 and homeland of the Métis.
