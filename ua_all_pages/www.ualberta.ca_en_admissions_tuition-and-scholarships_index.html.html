Tuition, Scholarships + Financial Support

Your education is an important investment, and we don’t want finances to be an added stressor while completing your degree. The best thing you can do is start with a plan, and we can help you do just that.

1. Understand Your Tuition and Fees

The cost of your degree will depend on several factors, including your program, living arrangements and course schedule. Understanding and planning for these costs will set you up for success throughout your studies.

International students should refer to our International Tuition and Fees web page.

2. Apply for Scholarships and Awards

The U of A administers over $53 million in scholarships and financial support each year, and we use a single application form to assess for all application-based awards.

3. Access Financial Support and Advising

In addition to need-based funding such as bursaries and emergency loans, we also offer student loan advising, workshops and more. Whatever your financial situation, we can help guide you to the right support.
